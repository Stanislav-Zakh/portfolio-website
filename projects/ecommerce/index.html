A Clean and Intuitive Shopping Experience

The platform's main view is designed for seamless navigation and ease of use. It consists of four key components: a search bar, cart status, a left navigation menu, and a product grid. The product grid supports pagination and features responsive product cards that change color, move slightly, and add shadows when hovered over, enhancing user engagement.

Effortless Product Discovery and Selection

Users can find products through the search bar by entering keywords or browse through categories using the left navigation menu. Once they locate an item, they can add it to their cart, and the cart status icon at the top left will instantly update to reflect the total quantity and price. The cart status icon provides quick access to the cart’s detailed view. Here, users can inspect their selections, adjust quantities, or remove items before proceeding to checkout. For convenience, users can add products to their cart directly from the detailed product view.

Admin Panel for Easy Product Management

The platform includes an admin interface secured with Spring Security API, ensuring that only authorized users can access it. Administrators can add, edit, or remove products and categories from the database without directly interacting with the database itself. This streamlined approach makes product management efficient and user-friendly.
